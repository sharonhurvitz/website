Bio

Sharon Hurvitz is a New York-based mastering engineer and composer. She works as assistant to Alan Silverman at Aerial Sound — one of the most decorated mastering facilities in the world, with over 100 Grammy-nominated records to its name.

Her mastering credits span an unusually wide range: from Maro's debut English-language album So Much Has Changed, to Robby Good's 25-song theatrical cast recording Dis-topia, to Rhymoi Music's SACD audiophile release The Legend of Taiko. That breadth — indie pop, musical theater, Chinese classical — reflects a sensibility shaped as much by her own artistic practice as by her engineering training.

As an artist, Sharon releases music under her own name. Her debut album Clangen Vol. 1: Newleaf is self-released and entirely self-produced — composer, performer, and mastering engineer in one. The album draws from the world of the Warriors book series, blending orchestral and ambient textures into a fully realized nine-track narrative.

Based in New York, she works internationally.
